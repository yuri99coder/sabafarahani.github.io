About

I am a master’s student in Computer Science and Technology at Wuhan University of Technology. My current research focuses on using large language models to annotate multimodal electronic health record (EHR) corpora.

My broader interests include scientific computing, modeling complex systems, and using computational methods to study structure–property relationships.

Research Areas

Multimodal EHR Annotation

Corpus annotation with large language models, machine learning, and data-driven research.

Computational Science

Scientific computing, applied mathematics, computational chemistry, molecular modeling, and electronic structure.
