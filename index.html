Academic Profile

I am Saba Farahani, a master’s student in Computer Science and Technology at Wuhan University of Technology. My academic interests focus on intelligent computation, computational modeling, and data-driven approaches in scientific and engineering problems. I am especially interested in the intersection of computer science, computational methods, and applied research.

My research activities aim to combine theoretical understanding with practical computational techniques to solve complex problems in science and technology. I am committed to continuous learning, rigorous research, and contributing to meaningful academic work.
